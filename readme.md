Welcome to Stink Project!
This is an ASCII type of turn-based game (for now) where you play as a character that just came out of nowhere.
If you are a fan of fantasy rpg and you don't mind having a bit or two of unexperienced comedians' line within it, give this a shot!
The final goal is simple, beat the evil scum and its stinky plans! (Although, I truly do have to apologize that this one haven't gone that far, just yet).
How far this journey would go? How deep is the stinky evil corrupts the world?
Well, even if I told you, you won't going to believe it until you see it yourself, aye?
For the sake of peace! and victory!
